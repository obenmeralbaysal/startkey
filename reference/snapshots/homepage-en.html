Frequently Asked Questions About Franchising

The cost of a real estate franchise investment varies according to the region selected, the office size, and the team structure.
At Startkey, we help you to set up a professional real estate office with minimal start-up costs.
We provide our franchise fee, which covers brand usage rights, corporate identity files, initial training, digital advertising materials, operational support, and technological infrastructure services, with a transparent structure.
Please see our franchise opportunities for detailed information.

To buy a real estate franchise:

  • Fill in the franchise application form
  • You will be invited to a pre-interview to help us get to know you and analyze the region
  • The contract process will be then initiated
  • Your office may now open and start operating

You will get one-to-one support throughout the whole process, including managerial mentoring, regional consultancy, and operational guidance from the day you launch your office.
Apply for a franchise today!

Certainly! Startkey will be there for you every step of the way to help make your business sustainable, not just at the beginning.

  • Continually updated training programs
  • Technical and digital infrastructure assistance
  • Performance, counselling, and region-based reporting systems
  • Marketing content, campaign examples, and field operations management.
  • Access to a cloud-based CRM system, team coordination tools, and automated workflows

You will be supported at every stage of your franchise process with performance monitoring, development support, and a cooperation network.

Legal documents required to open a real estate franchise office are as follows:

  • Tax certificate
  • Business license
  • Vocational qualification certificates required for real estate consultancy (Level 5 VQA)
  • Immovable Property Trading Authorization Certificate (TTBS) 

Startkey will assist you in obtaining these documents and provide guidance throughout the application process.
We ensure that you make a safe start in accordance with the relevant legislation.

A franchise agreement is an official document that clearly determines the rights and responsibilities of the parties involved.
The contracts arranged by Startkey include the following key items:

  • Trademark usage rights
  • Guarantee of zone exclusivity
  • The extent of training and operational support
  • Financial terms and liabilities

Furthermore, following the signing of the contract, regional growth planning, consultant staffing support, and office development are also organized in line with strategic goals.
The entire process is presented to you in a way that's totally transparent and easy to comprehend. Our approach is to lay strong foundations for a lasting business partnership.
