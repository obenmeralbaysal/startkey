About Us

Startkey Turkey

Startkey is a real estate franchise brand based in Izmir. Startkey, one of the leading companies in the real estate sector, is the Turkish brand with the highest number of registrations in 35 countries. As "Türkiye's Real Estate Brand," Startkey reflects its global aspirations as strongly as its local strength through its ever-expanding and evolving service network.

Startkey’s remarkable growth story continues to gain attention, as it enhances its influence in the sector with over +80 offices. The steadily expanding, high-quality office network increases Startkey's capacity to provide more comprehensive services to clients and address real estate needs across diverse regions.

At Startkey, our focus extends beyond mere growth goals; we place great importance on education within the sector. Training is a fundamental element for enhancing the professionalism of our team and ensuring the delivery of exceptional service to our clients. We organize various training programs to keep our staff up-to-date with the latest knowledge, enabling us to support our clients with the highest quality and most current information.

 

Our Code of Ethics

Startkey professionals will participate in vocational training activities to advance their careers and sustain their sectoral growth.

Startkey professionals will stay up-to-date on all current issues affecting the real estate sector in their community, region, or city.

Startkey professionals will be knowledgeable about real estate laws and adhere to them.

Startkey professionals will not misuse their position or authority to gain unfair advantages over colleagues.

Startkey professionals will always protect their clients' interests and act honestly and fairly with all parties involved in transactions.

Startkey professionals will not mislead clients or engage in deceptive or unethical professional practices.

Startkey professionals will recommend legal consultation to clients when necessary.

Startkey professionals will disclose all facts about a property without distortion and reveal any defects if present.

Startkey professionals will relay all offers on listed properties objectively and promptly to the represented party.

Startkey professionals will not misguide property owners about market value to secure a listing.

Startkey professionals will not take responsibility for consulting or services outside their area of expertise.

Startkey professionals will deposit all client payments into the office owner's account under an escrow arrangement.

The Startkey office name and phone number must appear in all property advertisements, marketing, and promotional materials.

If a Startkey professional has a personal interest in a property, they must disclose it to all parties at the first meeting.

Startkey professionals will not place "For Sale," "For Rent," or similar signs on a property without the owner's consent.

Startkey professionals will not make agreements to collect multiple service fees for the same transaction without the parties' knowledge.

Startkey professionals will cooperate and share information with other office owners and consultants under the brand.

Startkey professionals may offer properties from the brand's listing pool to their clients.

Startkey professionals must obtain written approval for internal referrals (re-referral) and collaborations (co-operation).

Startkey professionals will avoid exaggeration and misrepresentation, providing clients with complete and accurate information.
